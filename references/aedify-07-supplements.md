# Supplements Guide

> Source: thefitness.wiki — The official wiki of r/Fitness and r/weightroom on Reddit.

## Core Principle

Supplementation should only be used for **additive effects**. Your primary fitness goals will be mostly achieved through diet and exercise. Supplements are not a substitute for proper nutrition and training.

## Recommended Supplements

### 1. [Creatine Monohydrate](http://examine.com/supplements/Creatine/)
- **What it does**: One of the only truly effective and completely safe legal supplements. Helps improve performance during exercise by increasing the body's capacity for high-intensity work.
- **How to take it**: 5 grams per day, every day (no loading phase necessary).
- **What to buy**: The cheapest monohydrate you can find. Fancy forms (creatine HCL, buffered creatine, etc.) are just marketing — monohydrate is the most researched and proven form.
- **Safety**: Extensively studied and shown to be completely safe for healthy individuals.

### 2. [Vitamin D](http://examine.com/supplements/Vitamin+D/)
- **What it does**: Has numerous health benefits if you are deficient, which many people in modern society are due to decreased sun exposure.
- **Who needs it**: People who spend most of their time indoors or live in regions with limited sunlight.
- **How to take it**: Follow dosing recommendations from your doctor or based on blood test results. Common supplementation range is 1,000-5,000 IU daily.

### 3. Omega-3 Fatty Acids ([Fish Oil](http://examine.com/supplements/Fish+Oil/))
- **What it does**: Good for general health if you aren't already eating fish regularly in your diet.
- **Important note**: Flax seeds and chia seeds are [NOT sufficient substitutes](http://examine.com/faq/can-i-eat-flax-seeds-instead-of-fish-or-fish-oil-for-omega-3s.html) for fish oil for omega-3s. The conversion of plant-based omega-3 (ALA) to the forms your body uses (EPA/DHA) is very inefficient.

### 4. Pre-Workout Supplements
- **What they do**: Can help with workout performance, but the majority of the effect comes from the very high caffeine content.
- **Consider**: Plain caffeine (coffee or caffeine pills) is a much cheaper alternative that provides most of the same benefit.

## What About Protein Powders and Mass Gainers?

Protein powders and mass gainers are **food, not supplements** in the traditional sense. They are simply a convenient way to meet calorie and protein goals. Key points:

- Whey protein has higher bioavailability than most other protein sources
- Their primary advantage is convenience — easy to prepare, transport, and consume
- They are not required and they are not magic
- They don't replace whole food in terms of nutritional completeness
- Use them as a tool if you're struggling to hit protein targets through food alone

See: [Should I drink protein shakes / whey protein / mass gainers?](https://thefitness.wiki/faq/should-i-drink-protein-shakes-whey-protein-mass-gainers/) and [What supplements are good to take?](https://thefitness.wiki/faq/what-supplements-are-good-to-take/)

## General Guidance

- For questions about specific supplements, [Examine.com](http://www.examine.com) is the gold-standard reference for evidence-based supplement information. Also see [r/supplements](http://www.reddit.com/r/supplements).
- Be skeptical of supplement marketing claims — the industry is poorly regulated
- Most supplements beyond the basics above have minimal to no proven benefit for healthy individuals eating a balanced diet
- "Fat burners" and similar products are generally ineffective or provide negligible benefits relative to proper diet
- No supplement can compensate for poor diet, inadequate training, or insufficient sleep

## Common Supplements That Are NOT Recommended

- **BCAAs (Branched-Chain Amino Acids)**: Generally unnecessary if you're eating adequate protein. Whey protein already contains BCAAs.
- **Testosterone boosters**: Over-the-counter "T-boosters" do not meaningfully increase testosterone.
- **Fat burners**: Minimal effect; money is better spent on quality food.
- **Most "proprietary blends"**: You can't verify what's actually in them or in what amounts.
